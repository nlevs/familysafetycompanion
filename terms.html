Cyber Pop Terms of Service

Effective November 18, 2026. Cyber Pop was called Family Safety Companion until November 19, 2026.

1. What the app is

Cyber Pop is an educational tool. It helps you look for warning signs in a message and take small steps to protect your accounts. It cannot tell you that a message is safe, it can miss scams, and it does not replace your bank, your phone company or the authorities. Always check through a phone number or website you already trust.

2. Your account

You create an account with Sign in with Apple, or with an email address or a phone number and a password. Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Tell us if you think someone else is using it. How we handle your information is described in the Privacy Policy.

3. Free trial and price

New accounts get a free 7-day trial. After the trial the plan costs $4.99 per month, charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

4. Using the app

Do not use the app to harm others, to break the law, or to send material you do not have the right to send. You choose what you send for a check, and we ask your permission each time.

5. No guarantee

The app is provided as it is. We do not promise that it will find every scam or that it will always be available. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that result from relying on a check result.

6. Ending your account

You can stop using the app at any time. To delete your account, open Help in the app and tap Delete My Account. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription with Apple; cancel it first in iPhone Settings. We may suspend an account that is used to abuse the service.

We will update this page, and the date at the top, when anything changes.
