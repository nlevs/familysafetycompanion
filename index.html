How does Check a Scam work?

Paste a message, or choose one screenshot or photo. You review exactly what will be sent. Only after you tap Check This Message does it go to an AI service that looks for scam warning signs. You get a short summary and up to three next steps.

This check can miss scams. It cannot tell you a message is safe. Always check with a phone number or website you already trust.

What happens to what I send?

We ask your permission every time before sending anything. Our server handles it in memory and does not store it. The AI company, Anthropic, handles it under its own data policy, described in our privacy policy. Your Online Safety Module answers stay on your phone and are never sent with a check.

How do I delete my data?

Open Help and Privacy in the app. You can delete your safety plan, clear the current report, or delete everything the app keeps on your phone. These cannot remove copies you shared with someone, or content already sent to the AI company.
