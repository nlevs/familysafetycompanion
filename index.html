Cyber Pop: Help

Cyber Pop was called Family Safety Companion until November 19, 2026.

Plain answers about the app, the price and your information.

What does the app do?

It helps you look for warning signs in a suspicious text or email, and gives you a short list of small steps to make your accounts safer. It is made for older adults and the people who care about them.

It does not watch your messages, calls or accounts. It only looks at a message or picture you choose.

What does it cost?

There is a free 7-day trial. After that the plan is $4.99 a month. Apple shows you the exact price before you agree to anything. The app is sold in the United States.

How does the free trial work?

When you start the trial you pay nothing for 7 days. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, Apple charges the monthly price to your Apple Account, and the plan renews each month until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, tap Cyber Pop, then tap Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel the plan. We cannot cancel it for you, because the payment is with Apple.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You can sign in with Apple, or use an email address or a phone number with a password. We never ask for your bank details, and we never ask for a code sent to your phone.

How does Check a Scam work?

Paste a message, or choose one screenshot or photo. You review exactly what will be sent. Only after you tap Check This Message does it go to an AI service that looks for scam warning signs. You get a short summary and up to three next steps.

This check can miss scams. It cannot tell you a message is safe. Always check with a phone number or website you already trust.

What happens to what I send?

We ask your permission every time before sending anything. Our server handles it in memory and does not store it. The AI company, Anthropic, handles it under its own data policy, described in our privacy policy. Your Online Safety Module answers stay on your phone and are never sent with a check.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap I forgot my password and answer the question you chose when you signed up. If you signed in with Apple you have no password with us.

How do I delete my information or my account?

Open Help in the app. You can delete your safety plan, clear the current report, or delete everything the app keeps on your phone. Delete My Account permanently removes your account from our service as well. Cancel your plan with Apple first; deleting your account does not cancel it.

These buttons cannot remove copies of a report you shared with someone, or content already sent to the AI company.

Who makes this app?

Cyber Pop is made by a small independent developer. It is not made by a bank, a phone company or a government agency, and it does not replace them.

Contact us

Email familysafetycompanion.help@gmail.com. We reply within a few days.

Please do not email us messages to check. Use the app, and check with a phone number or website you already trust.

If you are in immediate physical danger, contact your local emergency services.
