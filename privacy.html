Cyber Pop Privacy Policy

First published November 3, 2026. Updated November 18, 2026 for accounts, the monthly plan, the learning streak and reminders, and November 19, 2026 to say that our plan service counts purchases. Cyber Pop was called Family Safety Companion until November 19, 2026.

1. Who we are

Cyber Pop is made and published under the name Cyber Pop. Contact: familysafetycompanion.help@gmail.com.

2. Your account

To use the app you create an account in one of three ways.

  • Sign in with Apple. We receive the email address Apple shares with us. You can choose to hide your real address, and Apple then gives us a private one it makes for you. We do not ask Apple for your name.
  • Email address. We keep the address and a protected one-way form of your password. We cannot read your password. We do not send email to the address to check that it is yours.
  • Phone number. We keep the number and a protected one-way form of your password. We do not send text messages to the number and we do not check that it is yours.

If you sign up with an email address or a phone number, you also choose a question (for example, the name of your first pet) in case you forget your password. We keep which question you chose and a protected one-way form of your answer. We cannot read the answer.

This account information is kept with our account service, Supabase, so you can sign in again. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. No tracking

The app has no advertising and no tracking identifiers, and we have added no tools that record which screens you open or what you tap. The one kind of counting to know about is for the monthly plan: RevenueCat (section 4) counts purchases, so we can see totals such as how many plans are active. The app does not read your messages, calls, photos or contacts on its own, and nothing runs in the background.

4. The monthly plan

The app offers a monthly plan with a free 7-day trial. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details.

To know whether your plan is active we use a service called RevenueCat. It receives your account's ID number (a random code, not your name, email address or phone number) and the purchase details Apple gives it. Our server keeps, next to your account, whether your plan is active, which plan it is, and the dates of your trial and your current paid period.

5. What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your phone, and we cannot see them: your Online Safety Module answers (including whether you have been scammed before and what your goals are), your safety plan, which steps you marked, how many daily lessons you finished, your daily scam practice numbers (how many practices you finished, your days in a row and your average score), your learning streak (which days you finished a lesson or the daily quiz), which reminders are on, and your settings.

The app can show three reminders: a daily practice reminder, a streak reminder at 7 PM on a day your streak is about to end, and one reminder the day before your free trial ends. Each one is set by your iPhone itself, and you can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings at any time. Turning a reminder on or off does not send anything to us, and we cannot see which reminders you have on.

Deleting the app, or using "Delete Everything on This Phone" in the app, removes this information. We do not make a cloud backup of it. If iCloud Backup is on, your iPhone's own backup can include it, like other app data.

6. What is sent when you check a message

Only when you tap Check This Message, the text you pasted or the one photo you chose is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to Anthropic, an artificial-intelligence company, to look for scam warning signs. We ask your permission every time. What you send may include personal information, so please remove anything you do not want to send first. Your Online Safety Module answers are never sent with it.

7. How long it is kept

Our server handles what you send for a check in memory and does not store it. We keep only counts (such as the number of checks, errors and costs) for up to 7 days, with no content.

Anthropic handles what you send under its own policy. Its page says "we automatically delete inputs and outputs on our backend within 30 days of receipt or generation", with exceptions such as content flagged under its usage policy (which it may keep longer) or when the law requires it (Anthropic Privacy Center, page dated July 1, 2026, checked November 18, 2026). We cannot delete content already sent to Anthropic. We do not claim that nothing is kept.

Your account information and our record of your plan are kept until you delete your account.

8. Sharing with someone you trust

"Ask Someone I Trust" makes a short text report on your phone and opens the iPhone sharing screen. You choose the app and the person. Nothing is ever sent automatically, and we keep no copy. The person you share with, and the app you use, control their copy.

9. Your choices and deleting your information

You can say no at the permission screen each time you check a message.

In Help and Privacy in the app you can:

  • delete your safety plan, clear the current report, or delete everything the app keeps on your phone;
  • sign out;
  • delete your account. This permanently removes your account from our service (your email address or phone number, your password, your password question and answer, and our record of your plan) and everything the app keeps on your phone. It cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription with Apple. Cancel it first in iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

These buttons cannot remove copies of a report you shared, content already sent to Anthropic, or the purchase record RevenueCat holds under your random ID number. To ask us to remove that purchase record, email familysafetycompanion.help@gmail.com.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

11. Changes

We will update this page, and the date at the top, when anything changes.
