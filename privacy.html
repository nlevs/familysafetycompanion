Family Safety Companion Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

1. Who we are

Family Safety Companion is made and published under the name Family Safety Companion. Contact: familysafetycompanion.help@gmail.com.

2. No account, no tracking

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking identifiers. It does not read your messages, calls, photos or contacts on its own.

3. What stays on your phone

Your Online Safety Module answers, your safety plan, which steps you marked, your first-launch choice and your settings are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them. Deleting the app, or using "Delete Everything on This Phone" in the app, removes them. We do not make a cloud backup of this data. If iCloud Backup is on, your iPhone's own backup can include it, like other app data.

4. What is sent, and when

Only when you tap Check This Message, the text you pasted or the one photo you chose is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to Anthropic, an artificial-intelligence company, to look for scam warning signs. We ask your permission every time. What you send may include personal information, so please remove anything you do not want to send first. Your Online Safety Module answers are never sent with it.

5. How long it is kept

Our server handles what you send in memory and does not store it. We keep only counts (such as the number of checks, errors and costs) for up to 7 days, with no content.

Anthropic handles what you send under its own policy. Its page says "we automatically delete inputs and outputs on our backend within 30 days of receipt or generation", with exceptions such as content flagged under its usage policy (which it may keep longer) or when the law requires it (Anthropic Privacy Center, page dated July 1, 2026, checked November 3, 2026). We cannot delete content already sent to Anthropic. We do not claim that nothing is kept.

6. Sharing with someone you trust

"Ask Someone I Trust" makes a short text report on your phone and opens the iPhone share sheet. You choose the app and the person. Nothing is ever sent automatically, and we keep no copy. The person you share with, and the app you use, control their copy.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can say no at the permission screen; the Online Safety Module still works. In Help and Privacy you can delete your safety plan, clear the current report, or delete everything the app keeps on your phone. These buttons cannot remove copies you shared, or content already sent to Anthropic.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

9. Changes

We will update this page, and the date at the top, when anything changes.
